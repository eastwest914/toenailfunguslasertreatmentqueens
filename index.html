FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about toenail fungus laser treatment at our Astoria Queens clinic.

What is toenail fungus laser treatment?

Toenail fungus laser treatment uses focused laser energy to penetrate beneath the nail and destroy the fungal infection at its root. The laser selectively targets the fungus without harming the surrounding skin or nail tissue. It is quick, non-invasive, requires no anesthesia or medication, and patients typically see improvement after just a few sessions at our Astoria Queens clinic.

How many sessions are needed to treat toenail fungus?

The number of sessions depends on the severity of the fungal infection and the number of affected nails. Most patients see visible improvement after just a few sessions. Our board-certified podiatrists will assess your nails at your initial consultation and provide a clear, realistic treatment timeline tailored to your specific condition.

Is the laser treatment painful?

Most patients experience little to no discomfort during the laser treatment. The procedure is designed to be as comfortable as possible. Some patients feel a mild warming sensation during the session, but this is generally very well tolerated. There is no recovery period and you can resume normal activities immediately after your appointment at our Astoria NY office.

What are the signs I have toenail fungus?

Signs of toenail fungus (onychomycosis) include yellowing, browning or whitening of the nail, thickened or brittle nails, crumbling nail edges, a distorted nail shape, separation of the nail from the nail bed and sometimes an unpleasant odor. If you notice any of these symptoms, visit Ideal Podiatry in Astoria for a professional diagnosis and laser treatment plan.

Why choose laser treatment over topical or oral antifungals?

Laser treatment directly targets the fungus beneath the nail without the side effects associated with oral antifungal medications, which can affect the liver and require blood monitoring. Topical treatments often cannot penetrate the nail deeply enough to reach the fungus. Laser therapy is fast, effective, and safe — making it the preferred choice for patients in Astoria and across Queens who want lasting results without medication.
